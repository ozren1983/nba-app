# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Overview

"Liam's NBA Cards" — a kid-friendly web app for learning NBA teams (flip through team cards, then take a quiz). Almost everything is in `index.html`: inline CSS, inline vanilla JS (ES5-style IIFE), and embedded audio. Alongside it are `logos/` (official team logo SVGs, from `https://cdn.nba.com/logos/nba/<NBA team id>/primary/L/logo.svg`), `apple-touch-icon.png` (the iPad/iPhone home-screen icon, also used as the favicon) and `tools/map/` (a Node script that generates the city map data; see City map below). The app itself has no build step, package manager, linter or test suite.

**Live site:** https://ozren1983.github.io/nba-app/ — served by GitHub Pages straight from the root of `main` (legacy build), so every push to `main` deploys.

## Running locally

Serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit http://localhost:8000 (opening `index.html` directly also works). Nothing is fetched at runtime except Google Fonts, and there is no live reload, so refresh after each edit. To try it on a phone or tablet (touch swipe, safe areas, audio), run `python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 0.0.0.0` and open `http://<mac-LAN-IP>:8000` from a device on the same Wi-Fi.

Because `main` is the live site, do work on a branch and merge to `main` only after checking it locally.

## Working with index.html

The file is ~4.7 MB because of two one-line JSON blocks just before the app `<script>`: `<script type="application/json" id="mapData">` (~200 KB, the city map, generated by `tools/map/build.mjs`) and `<script type="application/json" id="voiceData">` (~4.4 MB, a map of phrase → `data:audio/mpeg;base64,...`). Find them with `grep -n 'id="mapData"\|id="voiceData"' index.html | cut -c1-80`. Never read or print those lines whole; read the file in ranges that skip them (CSS, then markup, then the app script after them), and edit with targeted string replacements.

## Architecture

- **Team data**: the `TEAMS` array at the top of the app script is the single source of truth. Each entry carries names, city, country/flag, the city's `state` (none for a team outside the USA) and `lonLat` (`[longitude, latitude]`), a `logo` path, an emoji `sym` that shows what the name means (with its spoken `symWord`), card colors (`bg`/`fg`/`edge`, applied as `--t-bg`/`--t-fg`/`--t-edge` CSS vars via `teamVars`), palette `colors`, `titles` (the years of its NBA championships; the card shows the count and says `titlesLine(t)` on tap), and the kid-level `means` explanation with its Croatian translation `meansHr`. Shelf, cards and quiz are all generated from it. Star players live in a separate `PLAYERS` map just below it.
- **Language (EN/HR)**: the header switch sets `state.lang`. Only the "Why <team>?" story is translated (`meaning(t)` picks `means` or `meansHr`); the heading and the rest of the UI, quiz and audio stay English. `renderLang()` syncs the switch; `renderStorySay()` hides the story's read-aloud button only for a Croatian story that has no clip on a device with no Croatian voice. The story element gets `lang="hr"`, which switches it to Baloo 2, because Fredoka lacks č, ć and đ. Any other Croatian text needs the same font treatment.
- **Team pictures**: `fillSym()` draws a team's logo on the card, the "Why <team>?" panel, the shelf and the quiz; the emoji `sym` appears only if the logo file fails to load. `picWord()` picks what to say or label for a picture: the full team name for a logo, `symWord` for an emoji. Tapping the card or "Why" logo calls `openZoom()`, which shows the `#zoom` overlay with a big logo; any tap or Escape closes it. Overlays go through `openOverlay()`/`closeOverlay()`, which handle Escape and return focus. Their backdrop is opaque on purpose (see iOS home-screen mode below).
- **Star players**: `PLAYERS`, keyed by team `nick`, gives each team two players with jersey number, flag, specialities (`good`), height/weight (Basketball-Reference's listed cm/kg), clubs, and award years (`titles`, `mvp`, `scoring`, `threes`; a missing list means never won). The "Star players" panel under the story shows a jersey drawn in the team's colours (`jersey()`); tapping one opens the `#player` card and says the name. Names are recorded: `say` holds a respelling so the English voice pronounces it right (its clip is keyed by that respelling). A Slavic name has `sayHr`, the real spelling, recorded by the Croatian voice; `sayPlayer()` says it instead whenever it has a clip or the device has a Croatian voice. Player names use the display font Graduate, which has č and ć; Google serves Graduate with basic Latin only, so a second font link requests those letters with `text=`. Players are listed under the team they're best known for, so Giannis stays under the Bucks although he now plays for the Heat (likewise LeBron under the Cavaliers, now with the 76ers, and Zubac under the Clippers, now with the Pacers).
- **City map**: tapping the city on the card (`#cCity`, with a pin icon) calls `openMap()`, which shows the `#map` overlay and says `mapLine(t)` ("Denver is in Colorado!"). `renderMap()` draws an inline SVG from `mapData`: a US view for teams with a `state`, with the state filled in the team's colours, and a Great Lakes close-up with Canada highlighted and a North America inset for teams without one (the Raptors). Every team's logo sits on the map as a button (the current one bigger, in a ring of its colours, with a city-name pill). Tapping another logo calls `showOnMap()`, which moves the card to that team and redraws the map. A tap outside the card, the close button or Escape closes it. The map stays light in dark mode, like the logo windows; on narrow screens it keeps a 640px minimum width and scrolls sideways (`centerMap()` brings the team into view).
- **Map data** (`tools/map/build.mjs`): run `npm install && node build.mjs` in `tools/map/` after adding a team or changing a `city`, `state` or `lonLat`. It reads `TEAMS` from `index.html` and rewrites the `mapData` line. Shapes come from the `us-atlas` and `world-atlas` packages, with Natural Earth lakes vendored in `data/lakes.json`, and are simplified to about half a pixel. For each team it lays out all logos with that team's bigger and moves it off a small state so the highlight stays visible. It places the city pill and the state name: inside the state where it fits, otherwise beside it with a line, preferring open water. Label sizes come from the app's fonts (`data/widths.json`, advance widths measured from Fredoka and Graduate). It prints any overlaps it finds; fix them before committing.
- **Modes**: `setMode()` toggles two `<section>`s, `learn` (cards) and `quiz`. One shared `state` object holds mode, card index, sound flag, language and the current quiz round.
- **Quiz**: `buildRound()` makes `ROUND_LEN` (8) questions: `ROUND_LEN - 1` distinct teams across the `cityToTeam` / `teamToCity` / `findTeam` types, plus one `makeCountryQ()` question about the first non-USA team (currently Raptors/Canada). It reshuffles so the same answer never appears twice in a row. Wrong answers come from `others()`, which only picks teams whose cities differ from the answer's and from each other's (Lakers and Clippers both play in Los Angeles). A star is earned only when the first try is correct.
- **Sound**: `say(text, lang)` looks up the exact string in the `voiceData` map and plays the pre-recorded clip; if there is no clip it falls back to the browser's `speechSynthesis` (an en-US voice, or a hr voice for `lang` `"hr"`). English clips are one voice, and the Croatian stories and Slavic names another, native Croatian one (see "How the clips are made" below), so nothing should reach the browser voice. `chime()` is a WebAudio tone for correct answers. The UI elements marked `.needs-speech` are hidden when neither audio path exists.
- **Persistence**: `localStorage`, keys prefixed `liamNba.` (`sound`, `lang`, `idx`, `best`). A home-screen web app on iOS gets storage separate from Safari's, so these values don't carry over between the two. All storage access is wrapped in try/catch.
- **iOS home-screen mode**: don't add `apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style`. Apple has deprecated `black-translucent`, and on iOS/iPadOS 26.1+ it makes the system draw a blur over the top of the page. Without it, iOS colours the status bar from the page. The fixed `.safe-top` strip (solid `--floor`, full width, at least 8px tall) gives iOS a solid colour to sample. Without the strip it samples the transparent fixed `.court` layer and shows a smeared band. `theme-color` matches the floor colour. iOS reads these tags when the app is added to the Home Screen, so verifying a change means removing and re-adding the app.
- **`window.claude.hot`**: the app began as a Claude artifact, and boot hooks into `hot.snapshot`/`hot.ready` when that object exists. On GitHub Pages it doesn't exist, so `boot({})` runs. Keep the fallback working.

## Gotchas when changing content

- **Recorded audio is keyed by exact text.** Phrases are built at runtime (for example `"Yes! The " + t.nick + " play in " + t.city + "!"`, `"That's the " + t.nick + ". Try again!"`, and the end-of-round `"You got N stars! ..."` lines). Changing any team field, template string or `ROUND_LEN` means those phrases no longer match a clip, and they silently fall back to the robotic browser voice. New teams or edited wording need new clips added to `voiceData` under the exact resulting strings. Each team has 12 phrases (full name, nick, city, `symWord`, `means`, the three quiz questions, the two "Yes!" lines and the two "Try again!" lines) plus its color names; global phrases are `"Which team is from Canada?"`, the 0–8 star results, and `"Sound on!"`. Each team also has its `titlesLine(t)`, its map line `mapLine(t)` (shared by the two Los Angeles teams), its Croatian `meansHr`, and one clip per player (keyed by `sayHr`, else `say`, else `name`): 294 clips in all. A team winning a title changes its `titlesLine`, so that clip must be re-recorded too.
- **How the clips are made** (all re-recorded in November 2026, so new ones match): voices from the `edge-tts` Python package. English uses `en-US-MichelleNeural` at `--rate=+9%`; Croatian stories and `sayHr` names use `hr-HR-GabrijelaNeural` at `+0%`. Each clip is trimmed (`silenceremove` at -50 dB on both ends), gained to -22 LUFS, given 80 ms of silence before and 100 ms after (`adelay=80`, `apad=pad_dur=0.1`), then encoded as 24 kHz mono 40 kb/s MP3. Some clips are recorded from different text than their key, which never changes:
  - So that "LA" is said as two letters, the two Clippers full-name clips are recorded from "L.A. Clippers".
  - The Croatian voice reads by Croatian spelling, so English words in `meansHr` are respelled as a Croatian says them: Lejkersi, Minesoti, esadea (SAD-a), Pejs kar, Indijanapolis, Indi petsto, Bak, Golden Stejt, San Francisko, Kavalir, Klivlendu, Niks, nikerbokers, Njujorku, Nagit, Kliper, San Dijegu, Detrojtu, Dizni Vorlda, Medžik, Meverik, Dalas, Hit, Majami.
  - In Croatian, any buzz spelled with a B is read as letters ("be-ze-ze"), so the Hornets' "Bzzz!" is recorded from "Zzzzzz!".
- Team `titles` and the players' awards and clubs are correct as of October 2026 (after the 2026 Finals). They need updating when one of these teams wins a title or a player changes teams or wins an award.
- A new team needs its `state` and `lonLat`, a rebuilt map (`tools/map/build.mjs`) and a recorded `mapLine`. A second team outside the USA would also need its own close-up view in the builder, because the Great Lakes view is drawn around Toronto.
- The footer text assumes 30 NBA teams in total (`TEAMS.length + " of the 30 NBA teams so far"`).
- Dark mode tokens are defined twice (under `prefers-color-scheme` and under `[data-theme="dark"]`), so update both together.
